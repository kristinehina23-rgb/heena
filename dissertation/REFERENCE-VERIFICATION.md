## Year and pagination notes (APA 7)

Where Crossref lists an online-first year and a later volume, the Chapter 2 list uses the **paginated issue** when that is the version of record:

- Bouckaert: online 2018; *RELC Journal, 50*(3) is 2019, pp. 439–456 → **2019**
- Leijen, Pedaste, and Lepp: online 2019; *British Journal of Educational Studies, 68*(3) is 2020, pp. 295–310 → **2020**

Other online-first years were kept as deposited (e.g. Rathert & Cabaroğlu, 2021, later assigned to *Language Teaching Research* 28(5); Z. Li, 2020, later assigned to *Pedagogy, Culture & Society* 29(3); Han, 2022, later assigned to *Applied Linguistics Review* 15(4)).

Page ranges confirmed from PDFs or ERIC/RePEc where Crossref supplied only a start page:

- Chen (2023): 30–36 (CCSE PDF)
- Song (2024): 13–20 (CCSE PDF)
- Yu (2015): 113–122 (ERIC EJ1082096)
- Zhao (2024): 26–38 (RePEc / Redfame PDF)
- Hanifa et al. (2024): Crossref article number **69** (not 53)

Article numbers are used where there is no page range (Bao, 2020; Ling et al., 2023; Toledo-Sandoval, 2020; Dao et al., 2025; L. Wang, 2022; Li & Li, 2021).

Yan and Weng (2026) is cited as advance online publication (*Language Teaching Research*; no volume/pages yet).
