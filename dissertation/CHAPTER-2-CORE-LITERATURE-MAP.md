## Purpose

Chapter 2 should not describe 240 studies one by one. The central argument is that Chinese-language materials become locally meaningful through **teacher-mediated recontextualization**. That process often *begins* with mismatch between standardized materials and local learners, continues through teachers’ adaptation and multilingual mediation, and is enabled or constrained by institutional, policy, and professional conditions. Mismatch is a starting condition. It is not the research object of the thesis (the object remains recontextualization and ecological agency).

Use about **20–30 Tier 1** papers in close discussion and **60–90 Tier 2** papers in grouped citations. The archive stays for transparency.

---

## Argument spine

1. Standardized materials create recurring problems of fit, but fit is not the end of the story (Rathert & Cabaroğlu, 2022; Toledo-Sandoval, 2020; Hanifa et al., 2024; Mohamad Uri & Abdul Aziz, 2024).
2. Adaptation is a professional response to mismatch (Rathert & Cabaroğlu, 2022; Hanifa & Yusra, 2023).
3. Material adaptation is a form of teacher agency, achieved rather than possessed (Biesta & Tedder, 2007; Priestley et al., 2012).
4. Localization is broader than textual modification; materials are recontextualized in a classroom ecology (Zhao et al., 2024b; Loo, 2026; Yasasri, 2024).
5. Translanguaging is one important localization mechanism (Q. Zhang et al., 2020; D. Wang, 2022; Puspitasari et al., 2024; Nan et al., 2023).
6. Agency is enabled and constrained by institutions (L. Wang, 2022; Biesta, Priestley, & Robinson, 2015; Khan et al., 2022; Azeem et al., 2022).
7. Pakistan is a focused case of this wider problem (Hanif, 2023; Azeem et al., 2022; Khan et al., 2022; Lu et al., 2024; Lu & Hanif, 2025).
8. The gap concerns **enactment**: existing Pakistan work identifies mismatch and recommends Urdu support; it does not explain how teachers recontextualize materials in everyday classrooms (Lu et al., 2024; Lu & Hanif, 2025).

## Writing rule

Close discussion: Tier 1 only. Grouped citations: Tier 2. Do not bring a paper into the narrative merely because it was found. Keep a paper in the archive when it is loosely related, repeats a stronger paper, is abstract-thin, is only background, or cannot be verified.

**Chapter 2 headings.** Three levels only. Origins / applications / debates / definition / significance are paragraphs, not `2.1.1.1`. Section 2.1.4 is the locked relation (mechanism / achievement / process-outcome), not driver / goal / practice. Empirical review is 2.2–2.8 by **reasons**. Gap is **2.9 only**.
