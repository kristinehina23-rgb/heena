## How a supervisor or journal can re-run the DOI check

```bash
python3 -c "import json,urllib.request; doi='10.1111/flan.12460'; print(json.load(urllib.request.urlopen(urllib.request.Request('https://api.crossref.org/works/'+doi, headers={'User-Agent':'audit'})))['message']['title'])"
```

Or open `https://doi.org/{doi}` in a browser.
