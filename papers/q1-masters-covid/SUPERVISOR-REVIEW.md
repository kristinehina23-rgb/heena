## Wrong or risky highlights (do not use these as slogans)

| Headline in the draft | Why a Q1 reviewer will reject it |
|---|---|
| **97.4% had psychological stress** | Almost a ceiling. Only 1 of 38 ticked “none.” That item does not discriminate. Lead with **36.8% very high**, not “almost everyone was stressed.” |
| **OR = 25.67** in the abstract without the CI | The 95% CI is **4.42–149**. Direction is usable; the number 25.67 is not a precise population effect. Always keep the interval. |
| **Investment** as if it were measured | The form did not ask identity, imagined return, or withdrawal. Stress, self-rated learning, and “can online replace campus?” are **proxies**. Norton would not call this an investment study unless that is said plainly. |
| **CSC students** | The questionnaire did not ask scholarship type. Say **Chinese-university language students**. CSC is context, not a sample label. |
| **When Chinese class moved home** (title) | **9/38 IPs were in mainland China.** Not everyone was in a Pakistani house that week. Title is still usable if the abstract says so (it now should). |
| **97% stress / 76% unstable net** as if a national Pakistan fact | Convenience sample, phones only, **63% iPhone**. This is sequential inequality (power, noise, login), not “no devices.” |
| **WhatsApp was the classroom** | WhatsApp was marked **available**, not “used for class.” The used-for-class column is n.a. |
| **Any internet problem = poor learning** | Table 4 in the analysis file: infrastructure *problem count* × appraisal is **ρ = .02, p = .91**. Speed matters; ticking many problems does not. Do not collapse them. |

## Concept (this is the Q1 problem)

*Language, Culture and Curriculum* wants three words together. Right now:

- **Language:** present (Chinese as an additional language, oral class).
- **Curriculum:** thin. We never name the course, university, textbook, weekly hours, or HSK target. Reviewers will ask “which curriculum?”
- **Culture:** thin. Household as classroom is cultural, but the draft still talks mainly about electricity and apps. Textbook culture is postponed to another paper, which is correct, but then this paper must say what *culture* means here: the Pakistani home as the site of a Chinese class.

**Investment** is the right conversation (Hajar & Karakuş, 2024), but RQ1–RQ3 are about conditions and attitudes. RQ4 is implications, not a data question. One umbrella question plus three empirical questions is enough.

Figure 1 draws a one-way chain (power → platform → home → investment → outcomes). In the file these layers happen together. Call it a reading model, not a tested path model. There is no SEM, and n = 38 cannot support one.

## Methods and numbers (checked against `results.json`)

Numbers in Tables 1–5 match the export. These still need a sentence in the paper:

1. **Scale direction.** 1 = a lot / very high / very good; higher = worse. Without this table, ρ = .65 is easy to misread.
2. **Tencent Meeting:** 71% used it for class, but only 58% marked it available “in my country.” That is not an error in the table; it needs a sentence (VPN, used while in China, or messy item).
3. **Four people (10.5%) said their country has no internet** and still submitted Wenjuan. They likely meant home broadband, not “no network at all.”
4. **Two different 2×2 tables give the same OR = 7.65.** That is coincidence (both are 17/10 vs 2/9), not a copy-paste of one result. Say so, or a reviewer will think we duplicated a row.
5. **Many tests, n = 38, no correction.** Label the associations **exploratory**.
6. **No ethics-committee number.** Honest, but LCC may still ask. Do not invent one.
7. **No interviews, no HSK, all phones.** Already in limitations. Keep them. Do not add quotes.
