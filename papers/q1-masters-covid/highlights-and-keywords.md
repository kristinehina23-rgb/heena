# Highlights and keywords

Target journal: *Language, Culture and Curriculum* (APA 7th). Reuse the same block for *ReCALL* / *CALL* with only the last highlight changed.

## Highlights (3–5 bullets, ≤85 characters each if Elsevier; T&F is more flexible)

- Pakistani CSC students learned Chinese from digitally unequal homes, not campuses.
- Home ecology (space, power, family, time zone) predicted stress more than “the platform.”
- 78% reported psychological stress; 73% did not feel proficient after the course.
- Speaking and listening collapsed more than writing under emergency remote teaching.
- Localisation of ICLE must include infrastructure, not only culture in the textbook.
