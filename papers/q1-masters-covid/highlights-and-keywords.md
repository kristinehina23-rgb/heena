# Highlights and keywords

## Highlights

- Research problem: online Chinese teaching as a delivery problem (time, platform, household and platform access).
- Exploratory Wenjuan sample, N = 38, mainly Pakistani respondents, February 2022.
- Contribution: localisation of delivery (time, platform, task, participation mode, assessment).
- Home “affects study very much”: 11/14 also reported very high stress, against 3/24 others.
- Worse reported speed associated with worse appraisal index (ρ = .65, percentile bootstrap 95% CI [.36, .84]).
- QQ and Chaoxing often marked unusable in my country; Zoom and WhatsApp marked available.
- Historically situated emergency-remote-teaching case, not evidence of current hybrid ICLE.

## Keywords

online Chinese teaching; localisation of delivery; digital inequality; emergency remote teaching; Pakistan; household infrastructure
