## 中文速览（给导师 / 自己）

你现在手里最完整的硕士实证材料，是 2024 年发在 *Arbor* 上的那篇：

> Rathore, H., & Cao, H. (2024). An exploration the challenges and impacts during COVID-19 for online Chinese learning in Pakistan. *Arbor, 11*(1), 194–201. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13267236

- 样本：**97** 名在中国高校就读、疫情期间在巴基斯坦上网课的学生（男 69，女 28）
- 方法：14 题李克特问卷 + 访谈，混合方法
- 问题：那篇是短文、理论弱、统计报告有误，而且**已经发表**。直接改题目再投 Q1 = 重复发表，会被拒稿，也有学术伦理风险。

本仓库做的是：

1. **用新理论重写**（数字不平等 + 语言投入 investment），不是改几个句子。
2. **纠正百分比**（原文有把人数当成百分比的问题）。
3. **明确还缺什么原始数据**（SPSS、题项原文、访谈转写）。缺这些，*System* / *CALL* 这类顶刊过不了审。
4. 给你一条**更快的 Q1 备选**：把博士论文第二章 / 第三章的「教材适切性 / 本土化」写成概念论文（不需要新田野）。

## Honest recommendation

**Do not send the COVID manuscript to *System* this week.** *System* (IF 8.0, JCR Q1) will desk-reject a COVID-19 emergency-remote paper in 2026 unless it has (a) a new theoretical contribution, (b) clean inferential statistics, and (c) a clear statement of how it differs from Rathore and Cao (2024).

**Best sequence**

1. **This week:** freeze the new theoretical frame (digital inequality × investment × home ecology). That work is in `manuscript.md`.
2. **You upload:** SPSS/Excel file, the 14 item wordings, and 6–8 interview extracts (see `02-data-needed.md`).
3. **Then submit Track A** to a Q1 journal that still accepts mixed-methods Global South CALL work:
   - first choice: *Language, Culture and Curriculum* (Q1, IF ~2.8)
   - digital-methods alternative: *ReCALL* (Q1)
   - stretch after reanalysis: *Computer Assisted Language Learning* or *System*
4. **Parallel Track B (often faster Q1):** conceptual paper on **material–context fit** of *HSK Standard Course* in Pakistani universities, from your PhD Chapters 2–3. No new survey required. See `plan-b-conceptual-paper.md`.

For 中山大学 / 新汉学计划 graduation, you need **SSCI or CSSCI**, not only “Springer” in the title. ICCC is ESCI. A Q1 SSCI paper from Track A or Track B is the degree-relevant output.
