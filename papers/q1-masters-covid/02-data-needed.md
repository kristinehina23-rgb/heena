# Data still needed to finish Track A

The manuscript in `manuscript.md` is written from the **published 2024 frequencies and interview themes**. That is enough for a working paper. It is **not** enough for *System* or *CALL*, and even *Language, Culture and Curriculum* will ask for item wording and quotes.

Upload any of the following in this chat (SPSS, Excel, Word, PDF, or photos of tables). Do not upload passport scans or 现实表现鉴定.

---

## Must have (otherwise the paper stays a preprint-level draft)

### 1. Questionnaire

- The **14 Likert items in full English** (and Urdu/Chinese if used)
- Response anchors (the 2024 paper used 1 = strongly agree … 5 = strongly disagree — confirm)
- Which items belong to which of the four scales:
  - Support strategies (3 items, α = 0.824)
  - Enthusiasm for online learning (4 items, α = 0.835)
  - Learning achievement (3 items, α = 0.824)
  - Prior experience with online learning (4 items, α = 0.83)
- Demographic questions: university in China, city in Pakistan, HSK level, years of Chinese, urban/rural, device type

### 2. Quantitative file

Preferred: `.sav` (SPSS) or `.xlsx` with one row per student.

Minimum columns:

- id, gender, age (if collected)
- item1–item14
- optional: university, province, HSK, years_learning, device, platform

With this we can:

- report **real** item means and SDs on the 1–5 scale (the 2024 Table 3 means of 16–25 are not respondent scores)
- reverse-code negatively worded items
- compute scale scores
- gender comparison
- optional regression: stress / proficiency ~ internet + home + economic + platform familiarity

### 3. Qualitative file

- Interview guide (the three questions in the 2024 paper plus any others)
- Transcripts or the master’s thesis chapter that contains them
- How many people were interviewed (the article never states *n* for interviews)

We will pick **6–8 short quotes**. Do not invent wording.
