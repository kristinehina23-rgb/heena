# Title for faster Q1 desk review

Q1 editors decide in seconds. The old title stacked three theory words (*platform geography, home ecology, language investment*) before the study even appeared. That looks heavy and slow.

**Use this:**

> **When Chinese class moved home: Pakistani students, infrastructure, and platform access**

Why it is leaner and easier to accept at *Language, Culture and Curriculum*:

- 14 words  
- Who + where first (*Pakistani students*, *home*)  
- Two findings that are actually in the data (*infrastructure*, *platform access*)  
- No COVID-19 in the title (the years stay in the abstract; 2026 editors are tired of pandemic titles)  
- Theory (investment, digital inequality) stays **inside** the paper, where it belongs  

**Short title (running head):** Chinese class at home
