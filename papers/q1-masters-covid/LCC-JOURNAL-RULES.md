## Does this journal accept this kind of paper?

**In scope: yes, if we sell curriculum + identity, not "another COVID IT survey."**

The aims and scope welcome:

- additional languages (Chinese counts)
- language learning inside and outside the classroom (Pakistani homes)
- identity, attitudes, and investment (Norton / Darvin)
- curriculum development, organisation, or implementation

They already published the investment agenda we cite:

Hajar, A., & Karakus, M. (2024). Three decades of research on the model of investment in applied linguistics. *Language, Culture and Curriculum, 38*(2), 175-206. https://doi.org/10.1080/07908318.2024.2435284

That paper asks for (dis)investment of international students in languages other than English. Our sample is that population.

**What they have not published:** a 38-person Wenjuan study of Pakistani CSC students, load-shedding, and blocked Chaoxing. So this is a gap, not a copy.

**Desk-reject risk:** N = 38, self-report, no interviews. LCC publishes many qualitative and conceptual papers. A small questionnaire can pass if we (a) do not claim a national census, (b) keep the curriculum claim in the title of the argument, (c) report exact tests honestly. *System* / *CALL* would be harder.

## Pattern of papers in this journal (follow this)

Typical empirical LCC article:

1. Title (who, where, curriculum or identity issue)
2. Abstract about 150-200 words (ours is now shortened)
3. About 5-6 keywords
4. Introduction that names language, culture, and curriculum together
5. Literature / theory (investment, identity, curriculum in use)
6. Methods (site, sample, instrument, analysis, ethics)
7. Results (tables in the text)
8. Discussion (what this means for course design)
9. Conclusion
10. Disclosure statement, funding, references (APA)

British spelling is used in many LCC papers (localisation, normalisation). We keep that.

ScholarOne: anonymous review. Upload a title page with names. The anonymized file should not say "the first author was an M.Phil. at CCNU" in a way that identifies her if the 2024 *Arbor* note already does. Acknowledgements can go on the title page.
