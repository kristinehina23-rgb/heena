## Track A — empirical paper from the master’s COVID data

**Working title:** Home conditions and platform access in a 2022 online Chinese questionnaire

**Now with n = 38 item-level data.** First target is *Foreign Language Annals* (ACTFL / Wiley; languages other than English; they have already printed emergency-online and Chinese studies). *Language, Culture and Curriculum* is SSCI Q1 but is not the official-fit home for a household/platform paper. *ReCALL* is a possible second choice because of platform geography. *System* / *CALL* still want a larger N or richer interviews.

### First choice (submit here first)

**Foreign Language Annals** (ACTFL / Wiley)  
- Official refereed journal of ACTFL; languages **other than English**  
- SSCI; JCR **Q2** Linguistics and Education (do not call this SSCI Q1). Official Wiley 2025 snapshot: JIF 2.0  
- Cares about classroom teaching, online world-language delivery, and pedagogical implications  
- Already printed emergency remote language teaching, online tasks, online anxiety, emergency Chinese character teaching, faculty perceptions, and online collaborative writing by Chinese-language learners. See `FLA-JOURNAL-RULES.md`.  
- Typical length: 4,000–10,000 words including references  
- Style: APA 7th; American English; Challenge statement; Pedagogical implications; ScholarOne double-blind review  
- Before final acceptance: at least one author must be an ACTFL member  

Why first: the contribution is whether an online Chinese course can still be *taken* (household power, network speed, platform access), not a new app and not a culture-curriculum argument. FLA has named that failure; this file measures the learner-side conditions.

### Second choice (if the paper is framed as CALL)

**ReCALL** (Cambridge)  
- JCR Q1, IF about 7.7  
- Wants technology, tasks, and learner data  
- Only go here if you can add: platform names (DingTalk, Tencent Meeting, Rain Classroom, Zoom…), task types, and at least one table linking infrastructure to skill outcomes  

### Stretch (only after SPSS reanalysis)

| Journal | 2026 IF | When it becomes realistic |
|---|---|---|
| *Computer Assisted Language Learning* | ~8.7 | Factor analysis + regression/SEM + interview quotes |
| *System* | ~8.0 | Same, plus a tighter theoretical punch and implications that generalise beyond COVID |

*System* and *CALL* will ask: “Why is this not just another emergency-remote paper?” The answer has to be: **South–South scholarship students, Chinese (not English), and home ecology as the unit of analysis.** That is still a gap. The methods have to match the claim.

## Track B — conceptual Q1 paper from PhD Chapters 2–3 (often faster)

**Working title:** Material–context fit: reconceptualising the localisation of Chinese teaching materials for Pakistani universities

**First choice:** *Language, Culture and Curriculum* or *Language Teaching Research*  
**Also possible:** *International Journal of Applied Linguistics*; *The Language Learning Journal*; *RELC Journal*

This track does **not** wait for Pakistan fieldwork. It sells the **retain / adapt / supplement** model and the three-layer fit framework (materials × users × programme ecology). Empirical confirmation becomes a later paper — which is also your PhD.

Do **not** send Track A and Track B to the same journal in the same season.
