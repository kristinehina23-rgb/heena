## 2. Pattern of papers in this journal (we follow this)

Verified from ACTFL’s remit, the Wiley journal homepage, and articles FLA has actually printed since about 2018. Re-open the live author-guidelines page before upload; Wiley can update the wording.

Typical order in *Foreign Language Annals*:

1. Title (the teaching/learning problem; language other than English is welcome)
2. Short title / running head
3. **The Challenge** — about 50 words. Practitioner-facing. Why the paper matters in a classroom or program. CSCTFL’s author note models this feature on FLA.
4. Abstract about **150 words**: context, question, method, main result, **at least one pedagogical takeaway**
5. About **5 keywords** (ScholarOne often uses a controlled list; pick language + topic + level)
6. Introduction (the problem, the gap, the research questions)
7. Literature review / conceptual framework (synthesis, not one-paragraph-per-study)
8. **Methods** (FLA/APA name): context, participants, instrument, analysis, ethics
9. Results, with tables in the text
10. Discussion
11. **Pedagogical implications** — required in this journal; actionable, sample-bound
12. Limitations
13. Conclusion
14. On the title page only: acknowledgments, conflict of interest, funding, data availability, ethics, GenAI, notes on contributors
15. References in **APA 7th**
16. **American English** (this is an ACTFL / US journal). Use *localization*, *program*, *enrollment*, *judgment*.

ScholarOne is **anonymous / double-blind review**. Upload:

- `title-page.md` (names, emails, acknowledgments, COI, funding, data, ethics, GenAI, contributor notes)
- anonymized manuscript (title + abstract + Challenge + body + tables + references; no author names)

Do not write “the first author was an M.Phil. at CCNU” in the review file. Put that only on the title page. Do not cite a 2024 note in the article.

**Length.** Official author-guidelines range previously read from the Wiley page: **4,000–10,000 words** including notes, references, and appendices. Preference in older FLA notes: no more than about 35 double-spaced pages. This manuscript is inside that range.

**Format.** Double-spaced, 12-point Times New Roman, 1-inch margins. Tables and figures numbered in APA style and placed near the first mention. The Word builder already uses this.

**“Proficiency.”** In FLA, reserve *proficiency* for ACTFL-rated proficiency (for example OPI). This paper uses **self-rated Chinese level**, not an ACTFL rating.

**After acceptance (not before).** FLA commonly asks for a one-page OASIS summary and encourages depositing instruments or de-identified data in IRIS. Prepare those only if accepted. At least one author must be an **ACTFL member before final acceptance**.

| Rule | What we do |

| Original work | Original analysis of N = 38. Do not reuse any other tabulated N. |
| All authors agree | Xu Yiru and Cao Huimin must read the draft, agree the journal, and approve every version. Listing someone without consent is misconduct. |

| Authorship is human only | No AI tool as author. |
| Disclose generative AI | Title page already names language editing only. FLA’s forthcoming policy (https://doi.org/10.1111/flan.70017) plus Wiley ethics require a statement if GenAI was used. Do not invent data, interviews, or *p*-values. |
| Ethics | Adult questionnaire; consent by submission; no names or IPs in the published file. No ethics number: do not invent one. |
| Competing interests | “The authors declare no conflict of interest.” |
| Data | De-identified file from the corresponding author. Files with names or IPs will not be shared. |
| Similarity | iThenticate / Turnitin on the Word **body**; exclude references. |
| Not under review elsewhere | Cover letter says this. Do not send the same manuscript to another journal in the same season. |
| American English | Pick one and keep it. FLA is American. |
| ACTFL membership | Required before **final acceptance**, not necessarily on the day of first upload. Do not claim membership that does not exist. |
| Tables / figure | Tables in the text. Figure 1 as a real graphic, not an AI-fabricated research result. |
