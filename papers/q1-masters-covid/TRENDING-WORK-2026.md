# Trending work this Q1 paper needs (and does not need)

**中文先看：** 2026 年编辑已经不想再收「疫情网课故事」。现在热的是三件事：课后技术常态化、生成式 AI、投资理论（尤其是非英语 / 留学生）。我们这份 2022 问卷 **没有 ChatGPT 题目**，所以不要硬写成 AI 论文。我们要站在热门旁边说话：校园软件在巴基斯坦打不打得开、家里有没有电、女生样本不够。这些在 2026 年还成立，因为 CSC、HSK、腾讯会议/超星还在用。

Do not bolt GenAI onto `publication_n38.csv`. Cite Darvin (2025) as a **boundary**.

## What is hot in 2024–2026 (and how we use it)

| Trend | What the field is doing | What *this* paper does |
|---|---|---|
| Post-pandemic **normalisation** | Tools stay in the physical classroom (Moorhouse, 2023; Moorhouse, Wong & Li, 2023) | Show that this corridor never reached that stage: power, noise, blocked campus apps |
| ERT as Zoom-copy of campus | Stockwell & Wang (2023): videoconferencing emulated F2F | Add **which** conference tool opened offshore (Tencent vs Zoom/WhatsApp) |
