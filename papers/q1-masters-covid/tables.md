# Tables for the Track A manuscript

All counts below are taken from Rathore and Cao (2024, Table 2). Percentages are **recalculated as count / 97**. The 2024 article sometimes treated the raw count as a percentage (e.g. “60%” for 60 of 97). That is corrected here.

Likert coding in the source paper: 1 = strongly agree, 2 = agree, 3 = neutral, 4 = disagree, 5 = strongly disagree.

Neutral frequencies were not printed. Only collapsed agree (1+2) or disagree (4+5) bands that the article reported are shown. Do not add fake neutrals.

Item stems are reconstructed from the Table 3 narrative. Replace with the official questionnaire wording when available. Marked `[approx.]`.

## Table 5. Qualitative themes (reorganised for the Q1 paper)

These are **re-clustered** from Rathore and Cao (2024, §4.2). They are not new data.

1. **Infrastructural exclusion** — electricity / load-shedding; unstable internet; no device of the expected quality.  
2. **Domestic learning ecology** — no quiet space; family interruption; Pakistan–China time zone.  
3. **Pedagogical interaction breakdown** — limited interactivity; language barrier when asking for clarification; shyness; lectures that assumed the previous (missed) session.  
4. **Affective consequences** — stress; loss of confidence; comparison with “more competent” classmates; disengagement (“just complete the time”).  
5. **Uneven skill development** — writing somewhat retained; speaking and listening not; outcome described as alphabet + isolated words rather than proficiency.
