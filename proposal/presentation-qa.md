## One sentence (open with this)

This study examines how Pakistani teachers of Chinese recontextualize Chinese-language teaching materials in classroom practice, and how their agency is achieved or constrained in the ecological conditions of their work.

## Three terms (do not mix)

| Term | Say this | Do not say this |
|---|---|---|
| Recontextualization | The mechanism: what happens to the published page in class | “Teachers localize the book” |
| Ecological teacher agency | An achievement in conditions: possible, restricted, or redirected | “Teachers have agency”; “agency means they are free”; “why they do it” |
| Localization | The Pakistan field’s name; here, the process or outcome when recontextualization orients materials toward a Pakistani classroom | A third theory; a pre-set goal; a better-lesson test; a new textbook |

**Why they do it = RQ2** (practical-evaluative reasons). It is not another word for agency. It is not the test of localization.

## What you should present

1. The problem is today’s classroom, not a future Pakistani textbook.
2. The gap is the missing join: this population, this materials object, observation plus page comparison.
3. Three questions: what they do; why they say they did it; how agency is achieved or blocked in nested conditions.
4. Qualitative multiple-case design. About 6 to 8 teachers. 2 to 3 lessons each. Pilot first.
5. Faithful transmission still counts. Restricted and addition-only work still count as agency.
6. HSK, where it appears, is a condition (exam, syllabus, widely used series). It is not the research object.
7. CNKI is still a limit. Do not claim a world first.

## What you should not present or promise

1. Do not say Pakistani teachers already localize or already recontextualize. That is the empirical question.
2. Do not offer a new textbook as the doctoral product.
3. Do not offer an HSK-fit score as the main result.
4. Do not offer a survey of localization beliefs. That design already exists (Lu et al., 2024; Naheed, 2026).
5. Do not use needs analysis or cognitive load.
6. Do not rank teachers as good or weak adapters.
7. Do not fill a refused observation with a questionnaire and still call it this study.
8. Do not read the speaker notes that used to say “motive decides localization.” That line is wrong. The notes are now corrected.

## Likely questions, and a short answer

**Q1. Is this just HSK textbook evaluation?**  
No. HSK Standard Course is widely reported in Pakistan (Naheed, 2026). In this study it is an institutional condition. The object is what teachers do with the materials actually in use.

**Q2. Why not write a localized Pakistani textbook?**  
That is a product question. Pakistan CFL writing already recommends Urdu annotations and local modules (Lu et al., 2024; Lu and Hanif, 2025). Those take years to reach a class. This doctorate observes the work already happening, or not happening, with the page in front of the teacher.

**Q3. How is recontextualization different from localization?**  
Recontextualization is the mechanism. Localization is one possible bearing of that mechanism: toward a Pakistani classroom. A teacher may cut a unit only to finish the syllabus. That is still recontextualization. It is localization only if the work orients the materials toward that classroom.

**Q4. How is “why” different from agency?**  
“Why” is RQ2: the reasons given for a move in that lesson. Agency is RQ3: whether that work could be achieved, restricted, or redirected in the conditions. A clear reason can still sit inside almost no room to manoeuvre.

**Q5. If teachers just follow the book, is the study empty?**  
No. Faithful transmission is a coded practice (Rathert and Cabaroğlu, 2021; Song, 2024). Restricted and addition-only agency still count (Yan and Weng, 2026). The study fails only if it cannot see practice.

**Q6. Why qualitative? Why not a survey of 50 teachers?**  
Lu et al. (2024) and Naheed (2026) already collected perception. Criado (2023) and Neupane (2025) show that reported change outpaces actual change. A Pakistan survey of localization beliefs would reproduce that gap as if it were the finding. The missing evidence is the hour plus the page.

**Q7. Is 6 to 8 teachers enough?**  
It is a case target, not a census. Precedent: Priestley, Biesta, and Robinson (2015); Guerrettaz and Johnston (2013); Dao et al. (2025). Depth and page comparison matter more than counting more teachers. I do not generalize to all Pakistani teachers of Chinese.

**Q8. Why 2 to 3 lessons, not a semester?**  
Enough to compare the page with more than one hour, after a pilot. A full semester per teacher would be stronger and may not be accessible. Access failure is reported as a limit, not repaired with invented data.

**Q9. What if a school refuses observation?**  
That case is dropped or limited. It is not recoded as interview-only perception research.

**Q10. What is new?**  
Not a new theory. The mapped literature has not joined this population, this materials object, and observation plus page comparison in one design. Alvunger (2018) already joined agency and recontextualization in Sweden. Yan and Weng (2026) and Dao et al. (2025) already observed textbook-mediated agency in EFL. The join for Pakistani teachers of Chinese is what is missing.

**Q11. Is this the first study in the world?**  
No. I will not say that. CNKI still has to be searched. C. Wang (2022) is still outstanding as a PDF.

**Q12. Why Bernstein if you are not doing sociology of knowledge as the whole thesis?**  
Bernstein (2000) supplies the mechanism: knowledge is selectively appropriated as it moves. Official recontextualization is already in the syllabus and the exam. The classroom is a further site. Yu (2015) uses the word more loosely, as local handling of reform. I do not follow that looser use, because then the materials disappear.

**Q13. Why Priestley / Biesta, not Bandura?**  
Bandura (2001) treats agency as individual capacity. A committed teacher in a tightly examined course can still have almost no room to change the sequence. The ecological account treats agency as achievement in contexts-for-action (Biesta and Tedder, 2007; Priestley, Biesta, and Robinson, 2015). Autonomy is a condition, not a synonym for agency.

**Q14. Why not Tao and Gao as the model?**  
Tao and Gao (2021) is a map of language teacher agency. It is not the working model. The working model is the ecological triad.

**Q15. Will you include Chinese national teachers?**  
The title population is Pakistani teachers of Chinese. If a Chinese national teacher appears in a site, that person is a contrastive case, not the title population.

**Q16. What about learners? They also recontextualize.**  
Learner difficulty is a condition teachers read (Aftab and Abbasi, 2024). Learner co-recontextualization is not the object of this study. Further research can take it up.

**Q17. How will you know a move is localization and not just any change?**  
Not by counting moves, and not by asking whether the lesson was better. By whether the recontextualization orients the materials toward that Pakistani classroom: learners, languages, cultural reference, institutional setting. Criado (2023) already showed that a busy move-count can leave the prescribed sequence intact.

**Q18. What languages in class?**  
Chinese, Urdu, and English are data, not noise. Translanguaging may be a means of moving the book into the class. It is not automatically localization.

**Q19. How do you avoid imposing China / Vietnam codes on Pakistan?**  
Analogues (Dao et al., 2025; Yan and Weng, 2026) are sensitizing. Within-case analysis comes first. Labels such as addition-only are used only if the Pakistani case supports them.

**Q20. Theoretical framework has two boxes. Where is localization?**  
Localization is not Framework 3. It is the field’s problem-name, read from practice in the findings.

**Q21. Why two findings chapters?**  
The title names two objects: materials recontextualization, and teacher agency. Chapter 5 answers RQ1. Chapter 6 answers RQ2 and RQ3.

**Q22. Sample across which cities / institutes?**  
Purposive variation in institution type, experience, and materials actually used, not a national map. I cannot name sites before access and ethics.

**Q23. Ethics and video?**  
Ethics and institutional access before fieldwork. Universities and students are anonymized. If video is refused, field notes and consented audio. Students are not the interviewees.

**Q24. How is trustworthiness built?**  
Triangulation of say, do, and artefact. Within-case then cross-case. Member checking of case summaries. Audit trail. No claim that the cases stand for all Pakistan.

**Q25. Timeline?**  
The slide shows six stages, not calendar months. If asked: literature and framework, then ethics and pilot, then fieldwork, then analysis, then writing. I will not start Stage 4 before Stage 3.

**Q26. You cite Iftikhar et al. (2024) and C. Wang (2022). Have you read the PDFs?**  
Iftikhar et al. is used as landscape. C. Wang (2022) remains outstanding as a PDF. I will not make close claims from unread PDFs.

**Q27. Khan is 2023 in some databases.**  
The verified APA year for that article is 2022 (DOI 10.1177/02627280221120328). Print issue 43(1) is February 2023. I cite 2022.

**Q28. Is the literature only English?**  
The mapped core is mainly English-language. CNKI is still required. That is a limit of the opening report, not a finished claim that Chinese-language scholarship is empty.

## If they push you off the study

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| Write the Pakistani HSK book. | That is a later materials project. This doctorate has to see classroom practice first. |
| Survey 100 teachers. | Perception is already collected. It cannot show the hour. |
| Add student tests / HSK scores. | Learner outcomes are not the object. They would change the title. |
| Agency means empowering teachers. | This study does not take “promote agency” as its aim. It asks what is possible in their conditions. |
| Localization means adding Pakistani culture. | Culture is one possible bearing. Time, exams, and language choice also shape the page. I will not pre-define a successful cultural add-on. |
