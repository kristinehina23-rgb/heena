REQUIRED DOCUMENTS 报到检查材料

For All Students 所有新生均需准备
Please prepare all of the following documents.
以下材料均须准备：
• Admission Notice 录取通知书
• Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW201/JW202) 《外国留学人员来华确认表》（JW201/JW202）
• Original Diploma/Degree Certificate 最高学历毕业证书及学位证书原件
• Original Academic Transcripts 最高学历成绩单原件
• Original HSK Certificate or Language Proficiency Certificate HSK证书或语言水平证明原件
• Original Passport; copies of passport page, visa page, and latest entry stamp page (1 each)
  护照原件及护照页、签证页、最后入境签章页复印件各 1 张

Additional Documents for Undergraduate Students 本科生额外材料
• Visa & Entry/Exit Records for the Past Four Years 近四年的签证及出入境记录
• Original Birth Certificate 出生证明原件
• Copies of Parents’ Passports & Permanent Residence Documents 父母护照及永久居留证明复印件

Additional Documents for Graduate Students 研究生额外材料
• Certified Copies of Diploma/Degree Certificate 学历学位证书认证件

Important Reminder 温馨提醒
Please make sure all documents are authentic and complete. Documents not in Chinese or English must be accompanied by a certified Chinese or English translation.
请确保所有材料真实、完整。非中文或英文材料须提供经认证的中文或英文翻译件。
