Required Documents for Registration

Please prepare all of the following documents. 以下材料均须准备。

For All Students　所有新生均需准备

  1. Admission Notice录取通知书
  2. Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW201/JW202)《外国留学人员来华确认表》（JW201/JW202）
  3. Original Diploma/Degree Certificate最高学历毕业证书及学位证书原件
  4. Original Academic Transcripts最高学历成绩单原件
  5. Original HSK Certificate or Language Proficiency CertificateHSK证书或语言水平证明原件
  6. Original Passport; copies of passport page, visa page, and latest entry stamp page (1 each)护照原件及护照页、签证页、最后入境签章页复印件各 1 张

Undergraduate　本科生额外材料

  • Visa & Entry/Exit Records for the Past Four Years近四年的签证及出入境记录
  • Original Birth Certificate出生证明原件
  • Copies of Parents’ Passports & Permanent Residence Documents父母护照及永久居留证明复印件

Graduate　研究生额外材料

  • Certified Copies of Diploma/Degree Certificate学历学位证书认证件
Important Reminder　温馨提醒
Please make sure all documents are authentic and complete. Documents not in Chinese or English must be accompanied by a certified Chinese or English translation.
请确保所有材料真实、完整。非中文或英文材料须提供经认证的中文或英文翻译件。
